どんなゲーム？

15年前に焼けた白峰総合病院。夜は0時から4時44分までを繰り返し、戻るたびに廊下と部屋のつながりが変わります。一人称視点で病院を探索し、変わり果てた患者や職員を倒し、強化を選びながら5つのフロアを抜けて脱出するホラーシューターです。

  • それぞれの後悔を抱えた5人の主人公。記録を集めると、それぞれの真の結末へ
  • 倒れるたびに組み替わる病院と、毎回変わる強化の組み合わせ
  • 一人でも、Game Center で最大4人の協力プレイでも遊べます

About the game

Shiramine General Hospital burned down fifteen years ago. Now the night loops from midnight to 4:44 a.m., and every time it resets the corridors rearrange themselves. Explore in first person, fight what the patients and staff have become, pick upgrades and break out through five floors in this horror shooter.

  • Five survivors, each with their own regret — collect records to reach their true endings
  • A hospital that reshuffles after every death, and new upgrade combinations every run
  • Play solo, or co-op with up to 4 players over Game Center
